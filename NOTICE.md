# PaintCraft — credits

PaintCraft is built on **3D Paintcraft** by **Ras-Maciej**
(https://github.com/Ras-Maciej/3DPaintCraft), released under MIT OR Apache-2.0.

The painting engine comes from that project. It includes:
- the wgpu PBR viewport and path tracer;
- brushes, projection and stencils;
- the layer, mask and fill system;
- smart materials and procedural generators;
- the mesh-map bakers and texture export presets;
- the MCP server.

PaintCraft adds a redesigned workspace on top of it:
- a tool strip;
- a viewport bar;
- Properties, Layers and Shelf docks;
- a new theme and the cheetah icon;
- a set of usability fixes.

The original license terms (MIT OR Apache-2.0) apply to the whole program.
